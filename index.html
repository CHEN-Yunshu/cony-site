Fees, changes and how to get started.

Message Cony↗
How are fees arranged?

Fees depend on the services, duration and arrangements involved. Before booking, I confirm my service fee, clinic or salon charges, and the terms for cancellation, rescheduling and additional time.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Please let me know as early as possible so I can contact the clinic or salon. Cancellation, deposit and refund terms are those confirmed before booking. If you are running late or cannot attend, please get in touch promptly.

Can I book interpreting only?

Yes. Tell me the appointment setting, location, languages and expected duration. I will confirm whether I can help, along with availability and fees.

I haven't set my travel dates. Can I enquire?

Yes. You can message me directly with your expected month and what you need. We will confirm exact dates before booking. If you use the form, enter a planned date and mention that it is provisional.
